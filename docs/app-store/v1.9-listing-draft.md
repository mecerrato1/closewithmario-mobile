## Description
Agent | LO | Connect is a mobile workspace for loan officers to manage leads and follow-ups from anywhere.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Leads: See your leads in one place, filter by status and source, and open any lead for its history, notes and contact details.
• Today: Start the day with callbacks that are due, unread messages and leads that need attention.
• Callbacks and reminders: Schedule follow-ups and get a reminder when one is due.
• Messages: Read and reply to text conversations and Meta direct messages tied to your leads.
• Quick Leads: Capture a new contact in the field with photos and notes, then match it to an existing lead or create a new one.
• Realtors: Keep a directory of your realtor partners and the leads connected to them.
• Calculator: Estimate monthly payments and closing costs for common loan scenarios.
• Voice notes: Record a spoken update on a lead instead of typing.
• Contacts: Save a lead to your phone's address book when you choose to.

SIGN IN
Sign in with your email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google. Face ID can sign you in faster on your device. An account is required. Create or manage your account at agentloconnect.com.

TEAMS AND BRANDS
Loan officers on the Close With Mario team use this same app and see their team's name and colors after they sign in. Your account determines which leads and information you can see.

Calculator results are estimates for planning only. They are not a loan offer, rate quote or commitment to lend. Some features, such as notifications, ask for your permission.

## App Review notes
UPDATE TO AN EXISTING APP
This is an update to our existing app (bundle ID com.closewithmario.mobile), previously named "Close With Mario Mobile" and now named Agent | LO | Connect. Existing accounts and sign-in continue to work. No account migration is required.

WHO USES IT
Two groups of loan officers use this one app, with the same features:
1. Loan officers on the Close With Mario team.
2. Other loan officers who use Agent | LO | Connect.
Before sign-in, the app shows the Agent | LO | Connect name and logo. After sign-in, the app asks our server which brand the account belongs to and shows that brand's name, logo and colors. The brand changes only the name, logo and colors. It does not change features, and it does not decide which data an account can access; that is determined on our server from the signed-in account. If the brand cannot be loaded, the app shows a "Couldn't load your workspace" screen with a Try again button.

ACCOUNTS
New accounts are created through our website, agentloconnect.com, which the sign-in screen links to ("Create one here"). The app also offers Sign in with Apple and Google. Account deletion is available in the app: profile menu > Profile Settings > Delete Account.

PERMISSIONS
Notifications: reminders for follow-ups and new messages. Camera and Photos: profile picture and photos for Quick Leads. Microphone: voice notes on a lead. Contacts: saving a lead to the address book when the user chooses. Face ID: faster sign-in.
Text messages and direct messages are sent only when the user presses Send in a conversation. The app has no in-app purchases.
